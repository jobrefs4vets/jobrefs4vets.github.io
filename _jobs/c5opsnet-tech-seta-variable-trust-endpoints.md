## Role overview

A confidential defense contractor is seeking a senior technical SETA to support a next-generation classified network resilience effort in Arlington, VA. This role provides expert technical direction for secure, variable-trust endpoint development and delivers timely, data-driven guidance to government stakeholders.

## What you will do

- Provide onsite support to the Advanced Resilience portfolio for variable trust endpoint and classified network development.
- Advise government stakeholders on secure endpoint design, risk-informed controls, and resilient network architectures.
- Coordinate meetings and assess technical performer progress against project goals.
- Prepare technical reports, briefings, and presentations for senior officials.
- Evaluate emerging technologies and contribute to resilient classified network services, endpoints, and architectures.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
- 8+ years of relevant experience in cybersecurity, network development, network security, or enterprise systems
- Experience with secure platforms, endpoint security software systems, and private key encryption
- Experience with decentralized services, authentication mechanisms, access control, and endpoint security
- Familiarity with variable-trust systems, Zero Trust, and continuous integrity
- Familiarity with system certification and accreditation processes
- Active Top Secret clearance
- Able to work fully onsite based on client needs

Desired qualifications:

- Master’s degree
- Experience with classified network accreditation and DoD cybersecurity activities
- Experience supporting covert operations, cyber operations, or Special Access Programs (SAP/CAP)
- Experience with Agile development methodologies
