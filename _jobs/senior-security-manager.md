## Role overview

A security firm is seeking a Senior Security Manager to oversee industrial security and cleared personnel operations at a classified facility in Alexandria, VA. The role ensures compliance with DCSA, NISPOM, and facility security requirements. 

## What you will do 

- Manage industrial security operations and cleared personnel processes. 
- Oversee classified material handling, storage, and destruction practices. 
- Maintain DISS, NISS, and related security system records. 
- Coordinate security training, insider threat awareness, and self-inspections. 
- Support audits, incident reporting, and corrective actions. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Security, Criminal Justice, Business, or a related field 
- 7+ years of experience in industrial security or facility security management 
- Knowledge of DCSA requirements, NISPOM, and security program administration 
- Strong leadership, communication, and attention to detail 
- Active Top / SCI clearance
