## Role overview

A leading defense contractor delivers high-impact, technical solutions to complex national security issues. With extensive business expertise and consistent growth, they are known for continuous innovation for government customers, in both the US and abroad.

Their Operations Research and Cyber Analysis team is seeking a passionate senior-level Military Operations and Systems Analyst focused on national security challenges. In this role, you will directly influence the future of U.S. naval warfighting by providing the rigorous, data-driven analysis that guides critical DoD and Navy decisions.

## What you will do

As a key member of the team, you will conduct rigorous, objective analyses that shape military plans and strategies. Leveraging advanced simulation systems, you will support the full acquisition lifecycle—from identifying capability gaps and developing requirements to assessing the operational utility of new technologies. Your responsibilities will also include analyzing threats to platforms and systems, evaluating technology development programs, and performing quantitative modeling of naval forces.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in math, engineering, physical science, operations research, data science, or a related field
- At least 15 years of professional experience in naval operations via industry, government, or military service
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to summarize and present complex analytical findings to a variety of audiences
- Excellent problem-solving skills with the ability to develop and apply logical, methodical analytic approaches
- Top Secret / SCI clearance and the ability to maintain it throughout employment

Desired Qualifications:
- Experience analyzing metrics and conducting operational or technical risk assessment or root cause analysis, especially in the areas of undersea warfare
- Experience directly interacting with, briefing, and advising DoD or armed service decision-makers
