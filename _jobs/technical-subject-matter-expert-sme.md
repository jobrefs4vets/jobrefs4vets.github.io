## Role overview

A national security contractor seeks a Technical Subject Matter Expert to provide onsite advisory support for DARPA research and development programs in Arlington, VA. This role supports technical program management, proposal review, and risk assessment across advanced science and engineering efforts. 

## What you will do 

- Provide technical advisory support to program managers and research teams. 
- Review proposals, briefings, and technical reports for feasibility and risk. 
- Translate complex technical issues into clear, actionable guidance for stakeholders. 
- Assist with program planning, technical assessments, and technology transition discussions. 
- Support coordination across scientific, engineering, and government audiences. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in a STEM field 
- 15+ years of experience in technical leadership, research advisory, or program-level support 
- Proven ability to evaluate advanced technology and research programs 
- Strong written and verbal communication skills 
- Active clearance
