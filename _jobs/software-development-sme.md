## Role overview

A defense contractor seeks a Software Development SME to lead Agile software development and DevSecOps advisory support in Chantilly, VA. This role supports secure ground system software integration, backlog management, and operational transition. 

## What you will do 

- Guide Agile planning, sprint execution, and software delivery processes. 
- Collaborate with stakeholders to translate requirements into development work. 
- Support secure software integration, testing, and deployment. 
- Prepare briefings, reports, and status updates for program leadership. 
- Help maintain technical discipline and operational focus across the development team. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Software Development, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline 
- 8+ years of experience in Agile software development, technical leadership, or secure software engineering 
- Experience with DoD/IC software development and DevSecOps practices 
- Strong communication and coordination skills 
- Active Top / SCI clearance with polygraph eligibility
