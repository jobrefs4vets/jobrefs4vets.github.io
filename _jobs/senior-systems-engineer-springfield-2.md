## Role overview 

A national security contractor seeks a Senior Systems Engineer to support GEOINT ground system integration in Springfield, VA. The role focuses on system interoperability, requirements capture, and program engineering support. 

## What you will do 

- Support GEOINT ground architecture and integration activities. 
- Assist with system requirements, traceability, and architecture documentation. 
- Coordinate with government customers and technical teams on system design. 
- Participate in engineering assessments, trade studies, and system reviews. 
- Help ensure program solutions meet mission and interoperability needs. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Systems Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline 
- 10+ years of experience in systems engineering or intelligence system acquisition 
- Experience with GEOINT or intelligence ground systems 
- Strong coordination and communication skills 
- Active Top / SCI clearance
