## Role overview 

A defense contractor seeks an Integration Engineer to support enterprise intelligence systems integration in Springfield, VA. This role focuses on interface engineering, network systems interoperability, and end-to-end mission solutions. 

## What you will do 

- Develop and validate integration requirements for intelligence systems. 
- Support interface control documents, ICDs, and operational integration plans. 
- Collaborate with systems engineers, architects, and software teams. 
- Analyze, design, and test network and system interoperability solutions. 
- Assist government stakeholders with systems deployment and modernization efforts. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field 
- 5-10 years of experience in systems integration, network engineering, or intelligence systems support 
- Experience with interface engineering and interoperability documentation 
- Strong analytical and communication skills - Active Top / SCI clearance with polygraph
