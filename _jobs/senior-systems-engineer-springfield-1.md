## Role overview

A defense contractor seeks a Senior Systems Engineer to support GEOINT ground architecture and intelligence systems integration in Springfield, VA. This role supports mission-critical systems engineering, requirements development, and modernization efforts. 

## What you will do 

- Assist with GEOINT systems engineering and ground architecture integration. 
- Support requirements development, documentation, and systems modernization. 
- Work with government and industry stakeholders to align technical solutions. 
- Participate in system reviews, trade studies, and integration planning. 
- Help ensure system reliability, resilience, and operational readiness. 

## Qualifications 

- Master's degree in Systems Engineering or a related technical field, or equivalent experience 
- 10+ years of experience in systems engineering or intelligence systems support 
- Understanding of GEOINT mission requirements and acquisition processes 
- Strong analytical, coordination, and communication skills 
- Active Top / SCI clearance
