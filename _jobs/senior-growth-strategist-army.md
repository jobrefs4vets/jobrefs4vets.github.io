## Role overview 

A national security contractor is seeking a Senior Growth Strategist to lead Army market capture and business growth efforts from Huntsville, AL. This role supports high-value proposals, customer engagement, and strategy development for complex federal defense programs. 

## What you will do 
- Lead capture and proposal strategy for Army-focused opportunities. 
- Coordinate market research, customer engagement, and competitor analysis. 
- Develop and execute win strategies, call plans, and capture management deliverables. 
- Support proposal development, including management volumes, technical volumes, and basis-of-effort content. 
- Prepare briefings and status updates for internal leadership. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Engineering, or a related field 
- 10+ years of experience in capture, business development, or proposal support for federal defense or Army programs 
- Strong written and verbal communication skills 
- Experience with Army acquisition processes and socioeconomic contract vehicles 
- Active Top / SCI clearance or eligibility
