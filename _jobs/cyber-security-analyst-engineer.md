## Role overview 

A defense contractor is hiring a Cyber Security Analyst/Engineer to support classified Navy acquisition programs in Washington, DC. This role leads technical guidance for systems security, anti-tamper protections, and secure engineering through formal review gates. 

## What you will do 

- Lead cyber systems engineering for classified defense acquisition programs. 
- Support program protection, RMF tasks, and anti-tamper/SCRM integration. 
- Collaborate with program managers, engineers, and security teams on secure design. 
- Assist with requirements, documentation, and technical reviews. 
- Ensure compliance with defense cybersecurity and acquisition standards. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical field 
- 5+ years of experience in cyber systems engineering, defense acquisition, or program protection 
- Experience with DoD RMF, anti-tamper, and secure system requirements 
- Strong communication and technical coordination skills 
- Active Department of Defense clearance
