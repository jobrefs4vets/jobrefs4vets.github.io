## Role overview

 A defense contractor seeks a Project Lead Systems Engineer to provide systems engineering and acquisition support for advanced space and hypersonic programs in Chantilly, VA. The role bridges program management, technical leadership, and integration oversight. 
 
 ## What you will do
 
 - Lead acquisition strategy and development of procurement documentation. 
 - Oversee system-of-systems integration and interface management. 
 - Provide technical guidance on space and hypersonic systems engineering. 
 - Support performance reviews, risk assessments, and trade studies. 
 - Mentor technical staff and coordinate multi-disciplinary teams. 
 
 ## Qualifications 
 
 - Bachelor's degree in Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field 
 - 15+ years of experience in systems engineering or acquisition support for complex aerospace or defense programs 
 - Experience with space systems, launch integration, or hypersonic technologies 
 - Strong technical leadership and communication skills 
 - Active Top / SCI clearance
