## Role overview 

A government contracting office seeks a Contract Admin Specialist to support acquisition and contract administration activities in Chantilly, VA. The role ensures accurate contract documentation, market research, and compliance with federal acquisition policies. 

## What you will do 
- Prepare and maintain contract files, modifications, and award documents. 
- Support data calls, reports, and government review processes. 
- Analyze market information and prepare acquisition planning materials. 
- Coordinate with contracting officers, program staff, and financial teams. 
- Ensure compliance with FAR, DFARS, and contract requirements. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Contract Management, or a related field 
- 4+ years of experience in contract administration, acquisition support, or federal contracting 
- Familiarity with federal acquisition regulations and contract documentation 
- Strong attention to detail and communication skills - Active Top / SCI clearance with polygraph
