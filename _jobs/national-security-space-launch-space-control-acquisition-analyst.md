## Role overview

A confidential defense contractor is hiring a National Security Space Launch / Space Control Acquisition Analyst to support a high-priority space portfolio at the Pentagon. This role provides acquisition guidance and analytical support for national security space launch, space control, and strategic intelligence efforts.

## What you will do

- Support the oversight of national security space launch and space control acquisition portfolios.
- Ensure alignment with cost, schedule, performance, and risk goals across the program lifecycle.
- Facilitate senior-level reviews and engagement with industry partners and government stakeholders.
- Develop acquisition strategies, white papers, and decision memorandums to support program execution.
- Lead rapid acquisition efforts and ensure compliance with milestones, fiscal responsibility, and timely delivery of critical space capabilities.

## Qualifications

- 10+ years of relevant experience in national security space launch, C4ISR, or space control/space operations
- Bachelor’s degree
- Experience with the DoD acquisition system and/or ICD 801 acquisition processes
- DAWIA/DAU certification in Program Management
- Experience supporting senior leaders at OSD, Joint Staff, intelligence community, or senior military staffs
- Excellent writing skills and ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment
- Active Top Secret clearance with SCI eligibility
- Able to work fully onsite based on client needs

Desired qualifications:

- Master’s degree
