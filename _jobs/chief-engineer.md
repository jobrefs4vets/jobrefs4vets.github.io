## Role overview

A defense contractor seeks a Chief Engineer to lead secure cloud and classified systems engineering for government acquisition programs in Washington, DC. This role emphasizes SAP/SCI cloud environments, enterprise architecture, and secure hybrid deployments.

## What you will do

- Provide technical direction for classified cloud architectures and SAP/SCI integrations.
- Develop and oversee enterprise operating models, governance, and security controls.
- Coordinate system design reviews, requirements, and implementation plans.
- Support cross-functional teams on Azure, hybrid networking, and secure data management.
- Advise leadership on technical risk, cost, and mission alignment.

## Qualifications

- Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
- 15+ years of experience in cloud engineering, secure systems, or enterprise architecture for government programs
- Strong knowledge of Azure, SAP/SCI, and hybrid cloud environments
- Experience with DoD/NISPOM security and classified program requirements
- Active Top clearance or SCI eligibility
