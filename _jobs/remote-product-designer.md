## Role overview

We are looking for a Remote Product Designer to create elegant interactions, visual systems, and experience roadmaps for ambitious digital products.

## What you will do

- Shape product direction through user research and design strategy.
- Produce wireframes, prototypes, and design systems.
- Work closely with engineering to ship polished experiences.

## Qualifications

- Proven product design experience in remote teams.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
- Strong sense of typography, layout, and interaction design.
