## Role overview 

A national security contractor seeks a Principal Platform Engineer to deliver a secure Azure-based platform for classified and SCI workloads in Colorado Springs, CO. This role carries responsibility for platform operations, security, and continuous compliance. 

## What you will do 

- Build and operate a secure Azure platform for classified workloads. 
- Implement DevSecOps practices, observability, and automated compliance controls. 
- Support platform deployment, scaling, patching, and secure operations. 
- Coordinate with engineering, security, and operations teams to maintain mission readiness. 
- Deliver technical leadership for cloud platform sustainment and improvement. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline 
- 8+ years of experience in cloud platform engineering, DevSecOps, or secure infrastructure operations 
- Strong experience with Azure, Kubernetes, GitOps, and compliance automation 
- Proven ability to lead technical platform operations in classified environments - Active Top / SCI clearance
