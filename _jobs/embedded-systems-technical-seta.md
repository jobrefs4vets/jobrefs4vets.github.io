## Role overview 

A defense contractor is seeking an Embedded Systems and Technical SETA to provide onsite support in Arlington, VA. The role focuses on secure embedded hardware and software integration, prototype development, and classified systems transition for advanced research efforts. 

## What you will do 

- Provide technical capture and assessment support for embedded hardware and software platforms. 
- Coordinate integration, testing, prototype development, and transition activities. 
- Advise government stakeholders on secure multi-node system design and classified endpoint architectures. 
- Prepare technical briefings, reports, and analysis for program leadership. 
- Monitor performer progress and ensure alignment with research objectives. 

## Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field 
- 8+ years of experience in embedded systems, hardware/software integration, or classified technical support 
- Experience with secure execution environments, TPMs, or trusted computing technologies 
- Strong communication and technical reporting skills - Active Top clearance
